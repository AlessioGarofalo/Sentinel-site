Passive means passive. Neither stage performs active scanning, login attempts, exploitation, or probing. Sentinel reads public vulnerability catalogues, exploit-prediction scores, certificate transparency logs, registration records, and DNS. That is a deliberate ceiling, not a roadmap item: a resilience tool that quietly port-scans your suppliers creates a legal problem for you and an incident for them.
Scored to survive an argument. Each finding carries a priority score built from exploitability and how far it reaches through your dependency graph, and a separate confidence score built from source reliability, how well it matched the asset, evidence completeness, freshness, and corroboration. The two are kept apart on purpose, because "severe" and "certain" are different questions. Sentinel is also deliberately conservative about what counts: buying from a vendor is not the same as running their product, so a commercial relationship alone never manufactures a vulnerability against you.

Company Intelligence & Cyber Discovery Pipeline
Sentinel can now build the company graph for you. Name an organisation and staged ingestion researches it from public evidence, proposing offices, facilities, warehouses, infrastructure and providers with a claim, source, URL, publication date and confidence attached to each record. The whole thing runs as a persistent five-stage journey, company assets, cyber, signal scans, macro and micro economics, and risk composition, with per-stage status, evidence metrics, run history, cadence scheduling and variance reporting.
