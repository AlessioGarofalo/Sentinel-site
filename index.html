How It Works

Five stages.
Running on a loop.

Sentinel is entity-first: the model of what you depend on comes before the feed, and everything after it is judged against that model. Five stages keep the model current and turn it into decisions.

01
Assets
Discover and revalidate sites, providers and relationships.
02
Cyber
Attribute the digital estate to those assets, then screen it passively.
03
Signals
Refresh news, chatter, search pressure and infrastructure evidence.
04
Economics
Official indicators joined to your own exposure and concentration.
05
Composition
The analyst composes evidence into risks and responses.

Not a one-off scan. Every stage reports live status, evidence counts and errors, the cycle reschedules at the cadence you set, and each pass records what changed since the last, so new findings arrive as proposed variances against a graph you already reviewed. A blocked source stays visible as an evidence gap instead of quietly leaving a hole.

Your dependency map
stays yours.

Most platforms send your queries, documents and analysis to cloud servers. In Sovereign Mode everything stays on your hardware and keeps working when the network does not.

Path-Aware Trade Performance Resolver
Paper trade outcomes are now resolved against actual OHLC bar history rather than a single latest price snapshot. The resolver walks each daily bar between entry and evaluation, checking whether the stop-loss or take-profit was touched first. When both levels appear inside the same bar, the stop is assumed to have triggered first, a conservative default that prevents overstated P&L in volatile sessions.
